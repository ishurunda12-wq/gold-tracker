How it works

  1. MCX price. Every 30 minutes a script reads the live price of the current MCX Gold Ten contract. This is the wholesale price of 10g of 999 gold in India.
  2. Jeweller prices. The same script reads the price of a 10g 24K coin from each jeweller's website.
  3. The maths. Gold value = MCX price / 10 x weight x purity / 999. GST is 3% of the final price. Markup is the final price minus GST minus gold value.
  4. History. Once a day a second script builds one year of prices from the GOLDBEES gold ETF, scaled to match MCX.
  5. Hosting. GitHub Actions runs the scripts and saves the results. This page just reads those saved files, so it is free to run.

Prices are for information only. Always confirm the final price on the jeweller's site before buying. Delivery charges and offers are not included.
